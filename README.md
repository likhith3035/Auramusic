<h1><a id="features"></a>Features</h1>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### 🎵 Playback & Audio
- **Search, browse and stream** anything available on YouTube Music.
- **Hi-Res lossless audio** — FLAC/ALAC from configured module sources, with YouTube Music fallback.
- **Gapless playback with true crossfade**, adjustable 0–12s.
- **Automix [Beta]** — DJ-style transitions with beat-matching and tempo-stretching.
- **Offline downloads** — save songs and albums with embedded metadata and artwork.
- **Local music library** integration.
- **Background playback & lockscreen controls** via foreground media session.
- **Apple-like synced lyrics** animation.

#### 🌙 Smart Sleep Timer & Listening Control
- **Custom Time Countdown** — hours and minutes duration with quick presets (5m to 2h).
- **Non-Abrupt Stop** — current song plays to its natural end before stopping; never cuts off mid-track.
- **Song Count Timer** — automatically stops playback after $N$ completed songs (1, 3, 5, 10, 15+).
- **Live Controls** — Pause, Resume, Cancel, +5m, +15m with live pill on player screen.
- **Smart Inactivity Detection** — optional sleep detection that gracefully pauses when you fall asleep.

    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### 🌐 Connectivity & Accounts
- **Sign in with your Google account** for personalized recommendations and library.
- **Spotify integration** — import and stream your Spotify playlists and Liked Songs.
- **Discord Rich Presence** — in-app login with live status, song title, artist, and progress.
- **Scrobbling** to Last.fm and ListenBrainz.
- **Listen Together** — synchronized real-time party listening with friends.

#### ✨ Visual Experience & Design
- **Animated album canvas** — motion artwork on the now-playing screen.
- **Word-synced lyrics** — word/syllable-level highlighting from multiple lyrics providers.
- **Frosted-glass UI** — translucent glassmorphic surfaces powered by Haze and Material 3.
- **Dynamic color theming** — palette dynamically extracted from current album art.
- **Queue Add Songs** — quick search and add songs directly to active queue with drag-reorder.

#### ⚙️ Controls & Tweaks
- **Per-network audio quality** — separate quality ceilings for Wi-Fi and mobile data.
- **Playback speed control** (0.5×–2.0×) and **skip silence**.
- **System equalizer** & Nothing OS Glyph support.
- **Stats for nerds** — audio codec, bit depth, sample rate, and buffer diagnostics.

    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
